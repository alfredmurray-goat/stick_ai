Skills
Reusable SKILL.md instructions for planning, reviews, and project workflows. Use /skill-name in your message, or let Lite discover enabled skills.
Finds packs in .claude/skills and .agents/skills in your project and home folder. Project and imported packs need review before use; changed instructions need a fresh review. Skill text does not grant permissions or automatically execute scripts.
Create or import a skill$ARGUMENTS is replaced with the text after the skill command. Skills are saved locally and kept by app updates. Shell preprocessing, forked agents and permission overrides from other apps are not executed.
