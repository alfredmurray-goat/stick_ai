// how it works

It asks before it acts.

  1. 01
    Plug in, open. The app for your computer is right on the stick. Nothing to install.
  2. 02
    Ask in plain words. Stellan explores the project you choose, and only that project.
  3. 03
    Say yes or no. Every edit and command shows the exact lines first. Your file stays as it was until you allow it.

// built in

Small stick, grown-up tools.

thinking

Watch it think.

Stellan's reasoning and every file it reads stream into the chat as it works, then fold into one tidy line: Thought for 12 s · 2 steps.

memory

Rests when you do.

After 10 quiet minutes it gives your computer its memory back. Your next message wakes it in seconds, conversation intact.

hooks

Hooks, like Claude Code.

Run your own command, or tell another app, when Stellan finishes or needs you. A hook can stop a step. It can never approve one.

web

Web search, if you say so.

It can look up docs and error messages with Actuent. It asks first every time, and only the search words leave. Your code never does.

How we measured, and the small print

Stellan-1 Lite: all 164 HumanEval problems, pass@1, greedy decoding (temperature 0), each answer run against the official hidden tests, on a 4-core CPU with the app's own settings: 120 of 164 passed.

Other models: scores as published by their makers: GPT-4 and GPT-3.5 in OpenAI's GPT-4 Technical Report (March 2023, 0-shot), Claude 2 in Anthropic's Claude 2 announcement (July 2023). GPT-4 was OpenAI's top model until GPT-4 Turbo (6 November 2023); Claude 2 was Anthropic's until Claude 2.1 (21 November 2023). Each company used its own prompts and setup, so treat this as a guide, not a strict head-to-head.

Model sizes: Stellan-1 Lite has 7.29 billion parameters, counted from its model file. OpenAI has never published GPT-4's size; ~1.8 trillion is the most widely cited estimate, from a July 2023 SemiAnalysis report, so "99.6% smaller" (7.29B ÷ 1.8T ≈ 0.4%) rests on that estimate. Anthropic has never published Claude 2's size and there's no reliable estimate, so we don't give one.

To be clear: newer cloud models from OpenAI and Anthropic score over 90% on this test (Anthropic reported 92.0% for Claude 3.5 Sonnet in 2024) and are far larger. stick_ai gives you 2023-flagship-level coding help, privately, offline, on your own machine. Stellan-1 Max's score is still being measured.

// questions

Questions.

When can I buy it?

It isn't on sale yet. Orders open in February 2027 with shipping across the European Union, and in May 2027 for the rest of the world.

How much is it?

€15 per stick in the EU, VAT included, and $17 in the rest of the world, plus shipping. It's a one-off price: no subscription.

Is it really better than GPT-4 and Claude 2?

On HumanEval, a well-known coding test, Stellan-1 Lite scored 73.2%. GPT-4 scored 67.0% and Claude 2 scored 71.2%, as published by OpenAI and Anthropic. Both were their company's best model until November 2023. Today's newest cloud models score higher still. What makes stick_ai different is that it runs privately, on your own computer.

Does it need the internet?

No. Stellan runs entirely on your computer. It only goes online to check for app updates, if you connect GitHub, or for a web search you allow (only the search words are sent, never your code). Everything else works offline.

My computer says it can't open the app. Is it safe?

Yes. stick_ai isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate, so your computer may ask once before opening it. Here's how to open it: it takes a few clicks and only happens the first time.

Does the AI get copied onto my computer?

No. The models stay on the stick. While the app is open they're read into your computer's memory, like playing a film straight off a USB drive. Nothing is installed.

Which computers does it work on?

Macs with Apple Silicon or Intel (macOS 13.5 or newer), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) and 64-bit Linux. Lite needs 8 GB of memory; Max needs at least 16 GB and runs best with 24 GB or more.

Can it work with my other tools?

Yes. Like Claude Code, it supports MCP servers (tools from other apps), skills (reusable instructions) and hooks (your own commands or apps that run when Stellan sends a message, uses a tool, needs your approval or finishes). Hooks can stop a step but can never approve one: changes always wait for you.

Do I pay for shipping?

Yes. Shipping is added at checkout and depends on where the stick is going.
