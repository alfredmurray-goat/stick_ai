Opening stick_ai

Plug in the stick, open the app for your computer, and click Open workspace. The first start takes a few minutes while it checks the AI models; after that it starts in seconds.

  • Mac: Stick AI
  • Windows: Stick AI.exe
  • Linux: stick-ai

If your computer warns you

stick_ai isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate, so your computer may ask once before opening it. That's expected, and it only happens the first time. Apps opened straight from the stick usually aren't flagged at all.

Mac“can't be opened” or “Apple could not verify”

  1. Click Done (not Move to Bin).
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway next to Stick AI.
  4. Open Stick AI again, click Open Anyway and enter your password.

On macOS 15 and later, right-click → Open no longer skips this check, so use the steps above. (Apple's guide)

Windows“Windows protected your PC”

Click More info, then Run anyway.

If Smart App Control blocks it instead, there's no “run anyway”: stick_ai can only run on that PC if Smart App Control is turned off in Windows Security → App & browser control. It's only switched on for some new Windows 11 installations.

Linuxit won't start

Right-click stick-ai → Properties → Permissions → Allow executing file as program, or run chmod +x stick-ai in a terminal on the stick.

Other questions

  • It's slow. Lite needs 8 GB of memory. The larger Max model works best with 24 GB; on 16 GB the app warns you first, because it can be very slow.
  • Unplugging. Keep the stick connected while you work, and close the app before you eject it.
  • Still stuck? Email support@localilabs.com and tell us which computer you're using.
