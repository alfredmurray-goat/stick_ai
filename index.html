How we measured, and the small print

Stellan-1 Lite: all 164 HumanEval problems, pass@1 with greedy decoding (temperature 0), each answer run against the official hidden tests. It runs on the CPU of a 4-core computer with the app's own settings: 120 of 164 passed.

Other models: scores as published by their makers: GPT-4 and GPT-3.5 in OpenAI's GPT-4 Technical Report (March 2023, 0-shot), Claude 2 in Anthropic's Claude 2 announcement (July 2023). GPT-4 was OpenAI's top model until GPT-4 Turbo (6 November 2023); Claude 2 was Anthropic's until Claude 2.1 (21 November 2023). Each company used its own prompts and setup, so treat this as a guide, not a strict head-to-head.

Model sizes: Stellan-1 Lite has 7.29 billion parameters, counted from its model file. OpenAI has never published GPT-4's size; ~1.8 trillion is the most widely cited estimate, from a July 2023 SemiAnalysis report, so "99.6% smaller" (7.29B ÷ 1.8T ≈ 0.4%) is based on that estimate. Anthropic has never published Claude 2's size and there is no reliable estimate, so we don't give one.

To be clear: today's newest cloud models from OpenAI and Anthropic score 95% or more on this test and are far larger. Stick AI is about getting 2023-flagship-level coding help privately, offline, on your own machine. Stellan-1 Max's scores are still being measured.

Questions

When can I buy it?

Stick AI isn't launched yet. Orders open in February 2027 with shipping across the European Union, and in May 2027 for the rest of the world.

How much is it?

€15 per stick in the EU, including VAT, and $17 in the rest of the world, plus shipping. It's a one-off price: there's no subscription.

Is it really better than GPT-4 and Claude 2, and smaller?

On HumanEval, a well-known coding test, Stellan-1 Lite scored 73.2%. GPT-4 scored 67.0% and Claude 2 scored 71.2%, as published by OpenAI and Anthropic. Both were their company's best model until November 2023, when GPT-4 Turbo and Claude 2.1 replaced them. Stellan-1 Lite has 7.3 billion parameters, about 99.6% fewer than GPT-4's estimated 1.8 trillion (OpenAI has never confirmed GPT-4's size). Today's newest cloud models score higher still. What makes Stick AI different is that it runs privately, on your own computer.

Do I pay for shipping?

Yes. Shipping is added at checkout and depends on where the stick is going.

Does it need the internet?

No. Stellan runs entirely on your computer. You only need the internet if you choose to connect GitHub.

Does the AI get copied onto my computer?

No. The models stay on the stick. While the app is open, they're read into your computer's memory, like playing a film straight off a USB drive, and nothing is installed on your disk.

Is there a subscription?

No. You buy the stick once and it's yours. No account, no monthly fee.

Which computers does it work on?

Macs with Apple Silicon or Intel, Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) and 64-bit Linux. Lite needs 8 GB of memory; Max needs at least 16 GB, and runs best with 24 GB or more.
