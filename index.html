Install the app today

The software is free and works now. Download it for your computer, put it on a USB stick or in a folder, and open it.

MacApple Silicon and Intel · disk image WindowsWindows 10 and 11 · 64-bit Linuxx64
  1. Open the releases page and download the file for your computer. Pick complete if you want the AI models included, or model-free if you already have them.
  2. Unzip it onto a USB stick or into a folder.
  3. Open Stick AI. On a Mac, right-click the app and choose Open the first time; on Windows, choose More info → Run anyway. The app isn't signed yet, so this warning is expected.
  4. Click Open workspace, choose a project folder and start chatting.

Questions

When can I buy it?

Stick AI isn't launched yet. Orders open in February 2027 with shipping across the European Union, and in May 2027 for the rest of the world.

Do I pay for shipping?

Yes. Shipping is added at checkout and depends on where the stick is going.

Does it need the internet?

No. Stellan runs entirely on your computer. You only need the internet if you choose to connect GitHub or download updates.

Does the AI get copied onto my computer?

No. The models stay on the stick. While the app is open, they're read into your computer's memory, like playing a film straight off a USB drive, and nothing is installed on your disk.

Is there a subscription?

No. You buy the stick once. The app is free, and so are its updates.

Which computers does it work on?

Macs with Apple Silicon or Intel, Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) and 64-bit Linux. Lite needs 8 GB of memory; Max needs at least 16 GB, and runs best with 24 GB or more.
