Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

stick_ai is built so your code stays with you. The assistant runs on your own computer, and this website keeps as little about you as it can.

Who we are

stick_ai is made by Localilabs, Denmark. We're responsible for the data described here. Questions or requests: support@localilabs.com.

This website

No cookies, no tracking, no analytics. The site runs no scripts and loads nothing from other websites; even the fonts are our own copies.

Hosting. The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web server, it briefly records technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, browser and the page requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. We don't use these records to identify you. See Vercel's privacy policy.

“Notify me” sign-ups

If you ask to be told when orders open, we store your email address, the region you chose (EU or rest of world) and when you signed up. We use them for one thing: to email you when orders open where you live. No newsletter, no marketing lists, and we never sell or share your email.

  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR article 6(1)(a)), given by ticking the box. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Where it's stored: with Upstash, a database service used through our Vercel hosting.
  • How long: until we've sent the launch email for your region, then deleted, and in any case no later than 31 December 2027. Sooner if you ask.

The stick_ai app

Your code, files and conversations stay on your computer. The AI models run locally from the stick. We don't collect usage data, crash reports or telemetry, and there are no accounts.

The app only goes online in these cases:

  • Update checks. The app asks updates.localilabs.com whether a new version exists and downloads it if so. That server, run on Cloudflare, sees your IP address and the request, like any website. You can turn automatic checks off.
  • Web search, only when you allow each search. If you approve a search, the search words (or the page address to read) are sent to Actuent, which returns results. Your code and conversation are not sent. Web search can be turned off in Settings.
  • GitHub, if you connect it. Only when you link a GitHub account, the app talks to GitHub to open or publish your repositories, under GitHub's own terms.
  • Tool servers you add yourself. MCP servers you configure receive only the calls you approve.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, to get a copy of it, or to object to how we use it. Email support@localilabs.com and we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet, or the authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If it changes how we use an email address you've already given us, we'll ask again first.
