Your private coding assistant.

It runs entirely from this stick, on your own computer. Your code never leaves it.

  1. Plug the stick into your Mac, Windows PC or Linux computer.
  2. Open the stick and double-click the app for your computer:
    Stick AI (Mac) · Stick AI.exe (Windows) · stick-ai (Linux)
  3. Click Open workspace, choose a project folder and start chatting.

The first start takes a few minutes while it checks the AI models on the stick. After that it starts in seconds.

Needs 8 GB of memory; the larger Max model works best with 24 GB. Keep the stick plugged in while you work, and quit the app before you unplug it.

If your computer warns you

stick_ai isn’t signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate, so your computer may ask once before opening it. That’s expected and only happens the first time.

Mac “can’t be opened” or “could not verify”

  1. Click Done (not Move to Bin).
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll to Security and click Open Anyway next to Stick AI.
  4. Open Stick AI again, click Open Anyway and enter your password.

Windows “Windows protected your PC”

Click More info, then Run anyway.

If Smart App Control blocks it, Stick AI can't run on that PC unless Smart App Control is turned off in Windows Security → App & browser control.

Linux won't start

Right-click stick-ai → Properties → Permissions → Allow executing file as program.
